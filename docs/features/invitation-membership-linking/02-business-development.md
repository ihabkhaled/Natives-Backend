# UN-P0-INV commercial impact

- **Commercial value**: onboarding is the precondition for every other feature.
  Until it works the delivered product has no addressable users beyond seeded
  accounts.
- **Audience**: all teams, all roles. No segmentation.
- **Contract / SLA impact**: none contractual; the defect is a functional
  blocker rather than an availability breach.
- **Adoption risk**: administrators who hit the dead end once distrust the
  invitation flow afterwards. The receipt says the invitation was sent, so the
  failure surfaces later and is attributed to the wrong step.
- **Enablement**: the support runbook in `docs/identity.md` now names the
  symptom ("accepted the invitation but has no team") and the repair command.
